Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, practical answers about how Parichay evaluates your resume, handles document data, and calculates role alignment.

Which PDF formats are supported?

Parichay supports standard digital, text-based PDF documents exported from Google Docs, Microsoft Word, LaTeX, or Canva, up to 5 MB in file size.

Why can't Parichay read my resume?

If your resume is a flat scan, a photographed document, or flattened raster graphics without an embedded text layer, PyMuPDF cannot extract readable characters. To resolve this, export your resume as an electronic PDF directly from your word processor.

How is the alignment score calculated?

Your alignment score is an explainable composite of 4 mathematical components: Technical Skills coverage (45%), TF-IDF cosine semantic similarity (35%), Soft Skills & practices (10%), and Resume Structure completeness (10%).

Does a high score guarantee an interview?

No. The score is strictly an objective alignment measure comparing keyword and semantic patterns with the job description. It does not predict recruiter decisions or guarantee screening pass-through.

Why might a skill not be detected?

Skills are extracted using our curated technology taxonomy and contextual pattern matching. If a skill is heavily abbreviated, misspelled, or implied without being named, it may not be detected.

Is my resume stored on disk?

No. Uploaded PDF files are parsed strictly in memory. We never save raw resume files to disk or retain them after the analysis request finishes.

Is my resume shared with an AI provider?

Raw resume documents are never sent to external AI providers. Only structured summaries (extracted skills lists and score metrics) are sent to the Google Gemini API when generating AI recommendations.

How can I delete saved analyses?

When signed in, go to the "My Analyses" section in your sidebar. Click the "Delete" button next to any historical entry to immediately and permanently remove it from the database.
