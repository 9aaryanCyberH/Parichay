Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026. Please review these terms governing your use of Parichay Resume Intelligence and Career Readiness tools.

1. Service Purpose & Scope

Parichay is an analytical career intelligence platform designed to help job seekers, students, and engineers understand how their resumes align with target job requirements. The application provides document readability audits, TF-IDF semantic similarity scoring, skill taxonomy extraction, and grounded AI guidance.

2. Nature of the Alignment Score

The alignment percentage generated by Parichay is strictly an analytical alignment metric comparing text patterns and keyword taxonomy overlap. It is not a guarantee of employment, an interview invitation, or a predictive candidate ranking. Hiring decisions remain at the sole discretion of employers.

3. User Responsibility & Accuracy

Users are responsible for ensuring that the resumes they upload contain accurate and truthful representations of their experience. Parichay's AI recommendations and suggested bullet enhancements must be reviewed by the candidate to confirm factual precision before submission to employers.

4. Account Integrity

You agree to maintain the security of your login credentials. You are responsible for all activities occurring under your account. Parichay reserves the right to terminate accounts that attempt to bypass system security or upload malicious files.
