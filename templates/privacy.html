DATA PROTECTION & TRUST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains clearly and accurately how Parichay processes your documents, manages accounts, and protects your career information.

1. In-Memory Document Processing

When you upload a resume to Parichay, our engine processes the document in-memory using PyMuPDF. Raw PDF resume documents are never permanently stored, cached, or written to disk. Once the alignment analysis calculation finishes and the response payload is returned to your browser session, the uploaded file bytes are immediately released from memory.

2. Account Data & Analysis History

If you create a Parichay account, we store only the information required to authenticate you and persist your analysis history:

  • Account details: Your name, email address, and a securely salted and hashed password (via PBKDF2/SHA-256). We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Analysis metadata: The computed match score, matched skill tags, identified gaps, target role title, and timestamp of analysis.

You maintain full ownership of your data. You can delete any saved analysis report at any time directly from the "My Analyses" tab in your workspace.

3. External AI Provider Interaction

Parichay includes an AI Career Advisor powered server-side by the Google Gemini API when configured.

What is transmitted: Only structured technical summaries—specifically the candidate's extracted skill lists, target job requirements, and computed score metrics. Raw PDF files or unredacted document files are never transmitted to external APIs.

Deterministic fallback: When external API keys are not provided or if the service is unreachable, Parichay automatically uses its verified, deterministic in-memory rule engine. We never simulate or fabricate live AI calls.

4. Cookies and Sessions

Parichay uses strictly essential HTTP session cookies for user authentication and CSRF protection. We do not use third-party tracking pixels, marketing cookies, or external analytics trackers.

5. Contact & Inquiries

For privacy inquiries or account data deletion requests, contact the creator at Aaryan Kumar on LinkedIn.
