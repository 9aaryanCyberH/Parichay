Your Resume Privacy & Document Safety

Parichay operates on strict privacy principles. We parse your PDF in-memory. Raw uploaded resume files are never stored or retained on disk.

If you create an account, only structured analytical metadata (skills, alignment score, and candidate name) is stored in your private history database to let you reload previous reports.
