BUILT FOR CAREER CLARITY

More than a match score.

A useful resume review should explain what is working, what is missing, and what you can do next. Parichay brings these insights together in one focused workspace.

Resume intelligence

Extract relevant information from a text-based PDF, including skills, education, experience, projects, and certifications where identifiable.

Explainable role alignment

Compare your resume with a specific job description. Explore the estimated alignment score and understand how technical skills, contextual similarity, soft skills, and resume structure contribute to it.

Skills gap analysis

See which requirements are supported by your resume, which relevant skills may be missing, and which additional capabilities you bring.

Actionable AI recommendations

Receive suggestions for improving resume bullets, emphasizing relevant evidence, addressing skill gaps, and preparing for technical interviews.

Document quality review

Review supported checks for resume structure, section completeness, contact details, and writing clarity. Describe these as document-quality checks rather than guarantees of ATS acceptance.

Analysis history

When authenticated history is implemented and enabled, revisit saved analyses and compare your resume's alignment with different opportunities.

WORKFLOW

A clearer path from resume to readiness.

Start with the experience you already have. Parichay helps you examine it against a specific role and turn the results into a focused improvement plan.

01

Step 01 — Add your resume

Upload a text-based PDF. Parichay extracts readable content and identifies relevant sections and qualifications. Scanned or unreadable documents should receive a clear explanation of what needs to change.

02

Step 02 — Define your target

Paste a job description or choose an available role preset. Include responsibilities, required skills, and qualifications for a more useful comparison.

03

Step 03 — Understand your alignment

Review the estimated score, matched skills, potentially missing requirements, and evidence found in your resume. Explore the score breakdown rather than relying on one number alone.

04

Step 04 — Plan your next move

Use targeted suggestions to improve descriptions, demonstrate existing skills more clearly, and identify learning or project work that could strengthen your profile.

Your career information deserves care.

Resumes contain personal information. Parichay processes documents responsibly, explains how information is handled, and avoids collecting or retaining more data than necessary.

Documents are parsed in-memory during active sessions. Raw uploaded PDF files are never stored or retained on disk. When authenticated, only structured analytical metadata is saved in your private history database to allow revisiting previous reports. Extracted text snippets processed by the Google Gemini API are handled strictly for response generation without retaining or training on your information.
